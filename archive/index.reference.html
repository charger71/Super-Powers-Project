Superman

Kal-El. The Last Son of Krypton — and the figure that opened the collection.

The Superman figure launched Kenner's Super Powers Collection in the spring of 1984, packaged on a blue starfield card back with a Power Punch action, a mini-comic drawn by Jack Kirby, and the tagline that would carry the whole line: the world's greatest super heroes. Two years and three waves later it had grown into thirty-four figures, a dozen vehicles, and a corner of DC continuity built almost entirely for the toy aisle.
