Creators

The writers, artists, and designers behind Super Powers

Overview

The people who designed, wrote, and drew the Super Powers line — 6 creators. Each page collects an overview, a biography, and every toy and comic credited to them in the archive.

Alex Saviuk

Veteran American comic book artist highly regarded for his lengthy runs on Spider-Man titles and his foundational 1980s illustrative work for Kenner's legendary Super Powers Collection. · 1 credit

Dick Giordano

Additional figure and packaging artwork alongside the García-López style guides.

George Pérez

Designed Cyborg and redesigned Lex Luthor and Brainiac for the toy line — one of the rare cases of creator royalties. · 3 credits

Jack Kirby

Comic artist. Plotted the 1984 Super Powers mini-series, penciled the 1985 series, and redesigned his Fourth World characters for the toy line. · 20 credits

Jayson "Toy Otter" Geyer

cofounder of Raving Toy Maniac and Action Figure Insider · 1 credit

José Luis García-López

Artist of the DC Style Guides that most Super Powers figure and packaging art is based on. · 1 credit
