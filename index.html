Superman

Kal-El · Clark Kent · The Last Son of Krypton

Superman launched the Super Powers Collection in the spring of 1984 — the figure that opened the pegboard and set the tone for everything Kenner released across the next two years. He came packaged on a blue starfield card back with a Power Punch action, a mini-comic drawn by Jack Kirby, and the tagline that carried the whole line.
