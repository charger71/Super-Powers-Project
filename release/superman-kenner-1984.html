Superman

Kenner Super Powers Collection · Series 1 · 1984

Photography

Notes

Launch figure of the Super Powers Collection, packaged on the blue starfield card with a pack-in mini-comic. Squeezing the legs together swings both arms forward. The mold went unchanged across the line's entire 1984–86 run.

Variations

  • Superman - 12 Back - Fan Club Offer

    Card

    Kenner released Superman on all three different card styles for the 1st series action figure assortment. One of the three, shown above, is the 12-back card containing the "Fan Club Offer". While the card front contains a text offer banner in the lower right corner, the card back lacks any offer information.

    Instead, promotion details reside inside the mini-comic premium's inside front cover. Proof of purchase submission was not necessary for this promotion's redemption. Instead, the offer required submission of the "application" cut from the mini-comic or a 3" x 5" card containing name, address, and birthday (the same information requested on the "application") and a $3.00 check or money order by June 30, 1985. The Fan Club Offer Kit contained: charter membership certificate, color sticker, newsletter, deluxe patch, color photo, and reduced rate DC Comics subscription information. Kenner continued this promotion on 2nd series "Fan Club Offer" card styles, however the expiration date located on the card front's banner was updated to read June 30, 1986. Kenner released all twelve 1st series figures on the 12-back Fan Club Offer card style and all twelve have been personally documented in carded form.

  • Superman - 12 Back - Free Poster Offer

    Card

    Kenner released Superman on each of the three different 1st series card styles. One of the three, shown above, is the 12-back card containing the "Free Poster Offer". While the card front contains a text offer banner in the lower right corner, the card back contains all offer details. Submission of two proof-of-purchases along with name and address by the December 31, 1984 expiration date entitled young Super Powers fans to a 16" x 24" full color poster featuring all twelve 1st series characters. The poster shipped folded in a brown manilla envelope.
