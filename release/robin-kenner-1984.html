Figure

Robin

Kenner Super Powers Collection · Series 1 · 1984

Notes

Robin rounded out the Dynamic Duo in the first wave and remains one of the more charming sculpts in the line, presenting Dick Grayson in his traditional red, green and yellow costume with a removable yellow cloth cape. The figure's Power Action Karate Chop is activated by squeezing the legs together, snapping his arm down in a chopping motion that suited the acrobatic partner far better than a straight punch. Robin shipped in heavy quantities alongside Batman and Superman, and Kenner's case-pack practices mean he is still readily available today. He is also one of the most frequently found figures with a replaced or missing cape.

Robin's removable cape is a single-layer yellow cloth piece mounted on a plastic collar, matching the style guide artwork of the era.

A 16-page full-color mini-comic was packaged with every carded Robin.

Collector' Notes:

Be cautious with double-sided black-and-yellow Robin capes offered online — those are modern custom pieces, not Kenner originals. The factory cape is plain yellow on both faces.
